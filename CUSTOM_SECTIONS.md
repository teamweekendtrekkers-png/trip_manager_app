# Add your own trip sections

Open a trip, choose **Website sections**, then select **Add section**. Enter a
heading such as **Why choose us** and choose **Create section**. Add items with
your description, an optional title and an icon. The existing What Makes It
Special section stays separate.

You can rename your extra section, hide it without losing the content, choose
whether it starts expanded, move it above or below other extra sections, or
remove it after reviewing the confirmation. Extra sections appear after the
standard trip information on the website and in mobile and desktop previews.
They belong to the selected trip. Shared defaults remain unchanged.

Save a draft to keep editing later. Preview the trip, then use Update Trip and
the normal publish review to put the changes on the website. Install Trip
Manager 1.8.1 or newer before editing trips that contain these extra sections.
